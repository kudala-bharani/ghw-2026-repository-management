# Mergington High School Code of Conduct

This policy is part of a fictional GitHub Skills practice project, prepared with AI assistance.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone in our school community.

## Our Standards
- Be welcoming and inclusive.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for students and the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes inappropriate content, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing private information without consent, and conduct inappropriate in a school setting.

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers clarify acceptable behavior, take fair corrective action, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this code.

## Scope
This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement
In the fictional school scenario, concerns go to the IT Club faculty advisor. Reports are reviewed fairly, and serious misconduct may be referred to school administrators. No real school or reporting contact is represented by this practice repository.
